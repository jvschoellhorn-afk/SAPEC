Position Management

Positions let an organization manage headcount independently of the people in it. Expect setup, hierarchy and synchronization topics.

Quiz this topic

Key concepts

  • Position hierarchy. Each position can reference a parent position. The resulting hierarchy is shown in the Position Org Chart with incumbents and vacancies.
  • Position Management Settings. An MDF object maintained in Manage Data that holds global settings such as synchronization behavior.
  • Synchronization. Position attributes (e.g. department) can be copied to the incumbent's Job Information. It is enabled in Position Management Settings and uses a business rule.
  • Position control. With full position control, positions are planned and created first and every employee is assigned to an existing position.
  • To Be Hired (TBH). Marks a vacant position that should be filled; with Recruiting integration it can start a job requisition.
  • Right to Return. Keeps the link to an employee's previous position so they can return after a temporary assignment or leave.
  • Mass changes. Mass changes for positions update a field across many positions at once; an MDF import is the alternative.

Common traps

  • The position hierarchy comes from the parent position, not from the manager field in Job Information.
  • Synchronization needs both the setting and a rule.
  • Position Management Settings is maintained in Manage Data, not in Provisioning.

Practice tasks

Do these in a practice or demo instance. Each one ends with how to prove the outcome, just like in the exam.

Build a small hierarchy

Outcome: Three positions exist: a team lead and two team members reporting to it.

Hints
  1. Create the positions in the Position Org Chart or Manage Data.
  2. Set the parent position on the two team-member positions.

Prove it: Show the three positions in the Position Org Chart.

Hire into a position

Outcome: A new employee is hired into a vacant position, and the position shows them as incumbent.

Hints
  1. Mark the position as To Be Hired.
  2. Select the position in Add New Employee.

Prove it: Show the incumbent in the Position Org Chart and the position in the employee's Job Information.

Position changes reach Job Information

Outcome: Changing the department of a position updates the incumbent's Job Information.

Hints
  1. Check synchronization in Position Management Settings.
  2. Check or create the synchronization rule.

Prove it: Change the department on the position and show the new Job Information record of the incumbent.
