Business Rules & Event Reasons

Business rules default, derive, validate and trigger. The exam (and every project) depends on putting the right rule on the right event.

Quiz this topic

Key concepts

  • Rule events. onInit when a page or block loads (defaulting), onChange when a specific field changes, onSave when the record is saved (derivations, validations), onView to control display, saveAlert to create alerts with an alert date, onPostSave after saving.
  • Rule scenarios. Rules are created in Configure Business Rules from a scenario, e.g. Basic, Event Reason Derivation or Trigger Workflows. The scenario decides which outputs are allowed.
  • Assigning rules. Employee data rules are assigned to HRIS elements or fields in Manage Business Configuration. Rules for MDF objects are assigned in Configure Object Definitions.
  • Events and event reasons. Events (Hire, Data Change, Termination…) are picklist values. Event reasons are foundation objects linked to one event; one event can have many reasons.
  • Event reason derivation. An onSave rule on jobInfo (and compInfo) that sets the event reason from what changed, so managers don't pick it.
  • Detecting a change. Compare a field with its Previous Value, e.g. Job Information Model.Location ≠ Job Information Model.Previous Value.Location.
  • Workflow derivation. A Trigger Workflows rule decides which approval workflow starts. It is assigned onSave to the HRIS element.

Common traps

  • Event reason and workflow derivation rules are onSave on the element, not onChange on a field.
  • onView hides or shows fields; it does not change data.
  • Checking that a field is not null doesn't tell you it changed — use Previous Value.

Practice tasks

Do these in a practice or demo instance. Each one ends with how to prove the outcome, just like in the exam.

Derive the event reason for a transfer

Outcome: When only Location changes in Job Information, the event reason is set to Transfer automatically.

Hints
  1. Create an Event Reason Derivation rule.
  2. Compare Location with its previous value.
  3. Assign it onSave to jobInfo in Manage Business Configuration.

Prove it: Change an employee's location without choosing a reason, save, and show the event reason in Job History.

Fill job data from the job classification

Outcome: Choosing a Job Classification fills Job Title and Pay Grade.

Hints
  1. Basic rule, onChange on the Job Classification field.
  2. Set values from the Job Classification object.

Prove it: In Change Job and Compensation Info, pick a classification and show the fields filling before you save.

Contract end reminder

Outcome: HR gets an alert 30 days before a fixed-term contract ends.

Hints
  1. Use a saveAlert rule with an alert date of contract end minus 30 days.
  2. Assign it to the element that holds the contract end date.

Prove it: Save a contract with an end date and show the alert record or the alert in the HR user's view.
