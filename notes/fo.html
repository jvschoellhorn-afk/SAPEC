Study notes / Foundation Objects

Foundation Objects

Foundation objects describe the organization, jobs and pay of a company. They populate the fields managers pick from in employee data.

Quiz this topic

Key concepts

  • Organization structure. Legal Entity, Business Unit, Division, Department, Location, Cost Center and similar objects describe where people work.
  • Job structure. Job Classification (with country-specific children, e.g. for FLSA status in the US) and Job Function describe what people do.
  • Pay structure. Pay Component, Pay Component Group, Frequency, Pay Grade and Pay Range. Pay ranges are typically defined per legal entity, pay grade and geozone and are used e.g. for compa-ratio.
  • Associations. Associations filter values: once a Division is chosen, only the Departments associated with it can be selected.
  • Legacy vs. MDF. Legacy foundation objects are maintained in Manage Organization, Pay and Job Structures. MDF foundation objects are maintained in Manage Data and imported via Import and Export Data.
  • Other foundation objects. Event Reason, Workflow (approval steps and approvers) and Dynamic Role are also foundation objects.
  • Import order. Import objects that others refer to first (e.g. Legal Entity before objects associated with it), and foundation data before employee data.

Common traps

  • Event Reason is a foundation object; Event is a picklist.
  • Employee data is imported with Import Employee Data, MDF objects with Import and Export Data.
  • Importing children before their parents fails on the references.

Practice tasks

Do these in a practice or demo instance. Each one ends with how to prove the outcome, just like in the exam.

Filtered org picking

Outcome: In Job Information, choosing a Division only offers its own Departments.

Hints
  1. Check the association between Department and Division in the object definition.
  2. Create a Division and two Departments assigned to it in Manage Data.

Prove it: Edit an employee's Job Information, select the Division and show the filtered Department list.

A pay range that fits

Outcome: Employees in pay grade GR-05 in your German legal entity get the correct pay range and compa-ratio.

Hints
  1. Create or check the Geozone, Pay Grade and Pay Range records.
  2. Link the pay range to legal entity, pay grade and geozone.

Prove it: Open Compensation Information of a matching employee and show the range and compa-ratio.

Country-specific job data

Outcome: A job classification carries a US-specific attribute.

Hints
  1. Maintain the country-specific child of the Job Classification record.

Prove it: Show the job classification record with its US child data in Manage Data.
