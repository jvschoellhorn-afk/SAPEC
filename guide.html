What "system based" means

SAP has been moving its certifications to practical exams since November 2025. In a system-based exam you get a set of tasks and access to an SAP system, and you complete the tasks within the time limit. Your result is based on whether the tasks were completed and on what the system shows afterwards, checked automatically or by evaluation scripts.

The exams are open book. SAP names the SAP Help Portal, SAP Learning and SAP Community as resources you can use. So memorising menu paths matters less than knowing which tool owns an outcome and being fast in the system.

SAP doesn't publish topic weightings for this format. The eight topic areas on this site follow the structure of SAP's learning journey and the earlier exam versions.

How to use this site

  1. Study notes — read the notes for a topic: key concepts and the usual traps.
  2. Trainer — run the topic quiz for that area until your mastery bar is green. It checks your understanding fast.
  3. Practice tasks — do the hands-on tasks at the end of each notes page in a practice system. They are written like exam tasks: an outcome to reach and a way to prove it.
  4. Review — clear your weak spots and take timed knowledge checks in the exam simulation.

Booking and costs

You book through the certification page on SAP Learning. All certification activities need an SAP Universal ID with two email addresses assigned. SAP offers two ways to pay:

  • SAP Learning Hub subscription — annual, includes four certification exam attempts, practice systems and the yearly stay-certified assessments.
  • Certification exam, two attempts — two exam attempts plus 10 hours of hands-on practice system access, without stay-certified assessments.

SAP doesn't show prices on these pages; check the SAP Store or your SAP contact. Certified partner consultants get provisioning rights after they pass.

Staying certified

Certification is valid for 12 months from the initial exam. To keep it, you complete a stay-certified assessment at least once a year, within a 90-day renewal window. That requires an SAP Learning Hub subscription.

On exam day

  • Read all tasks before you start and write down the outcome each one asks for.
  • Make sure the system and setup guide work before you begin the timed part. The clock keeps running through breaks.
  • Leave visible proof in the system: a saved record, a Job History entry, a pending workflow. Configuration alone may not show that the outcome works.
  • Test both sides of any condition, e.g. the employee the rule should affect and one it shouldn't.
  • Keep the SAP Help Portal open in a tab — looking things up is allowed.
